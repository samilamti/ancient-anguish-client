A big update for players who live at the command line.

NAVIGATION COMPASS (Mac)
A compass rose sits over the terminal and reads your live coordinates, showing named landmarks within five stadia at their true bearing — with the nearest one always called out. Built from the official accessible map.

TAP-ABLE HINTS (iPhone & iPad)
The desktop TAB-completion you already know, now on touch. Start typing and matching commands appear above the input; tap one to send it straight to the game. Half-finished templates fill the field instead so you can complete them.

TARGETS AS LINKS
Creatures announced in a room now render as tappable links — tap to attack, no typing. The Kill picker seeds itself from whatever is actually standing in front of you, and lets you pin the targets you hunt often.

RICHER COMMAND HISTORY
Recent commands keeps sixteen entries in a scrollable list, and re-running one moves it back to the top. Counterparts sit above the list: send "enter portal" and it offers "leave portal" and "out"; open a door and it offers to close it — from either side.

TEXT LINK RULES
Turn any line the game prints into a one-tap command. Select text in the output to seed a new rule, use capture groups to pull words out of the line, and chain several commands into one link.

QUALITY OF LIFE
• Configuration panes open as docked panels on wide screens instead of covering the app
• Escape closes any panel or editor
• Tapping the output clears a selection, and tapping a link puts the keyboard away
• Command history no longer loses entries typed during startup
