The biggest update yet for players who live at the command line.

BATTLE, WITHOUT THE WALL OF TEXT
Combat spam can collapse in place, every blow overwriting the last, or leave the log entirely for a battle panel that tracks your hits, misses, evasion rate and current target. The line you were reading stops getting buried.

NAVIGATION COMPASS
A compass rose floats over the terminal on iPhone, iPad and Mac, reading your live coordinates and showing named landmarks at their true bearing. Built from the official accessible map, with labels that step apart instead of piling into a smudge in a crowded town.

SKILLS, SCORE AND SHOP LISTS AS PANELS
"skills", "score" and a shop's "list" arrive as laid-out panels instead of raw columns. Score shows what has changed since you last looked, and every item on a shop list is one tap from being bought.

TARGETS AS LINKS
Creatures announced in a room render as tappable links: tap to attack, no typing. The Kill picker seeds itself from whatever is standing in front of you and lets you pin the targets you hunt often. Long-press to stop a word being highlighted.

TAP-ABLE HINTS (iPhone & iPad)
The desktop TAB-completion you already know, now on touch. Start typing and matching commands appear above the input; tap one to send it. Half-finished templates fill the field instead so you can complete them.

RICHER COMMAND HISTORY
Sixteen entries in a scrollable list, and re-running one moves it back to the top. Counterparts sit above the list: send "enter portal" and it offers "leave portal" and "out"; open a door and it offers to close it.

TEXT LINK RULES
Turn any line the game prints into a one-tap command. Select text in the output to seed a new rule, use capture groups to pull words out of the line, and chain several commands into one link.

A SOUNDTRACK THAT KNOWS HOW YOU ARE DOING
Area music picks up tempo as your health falls, and battle themes hold back until a fight is genuinely under way.

QUALITY OF LIFE
• Configuration panes open as docked panels on wide screens instead of covering the app
• Escape closes any panel or editor
• Messages appear at the top of the output and fade on their own
• Optional extra line spacing, and text selection that works in the history view
• Tapping the output clears a selection, and tapping a link puts the keyboard away
