SP YOU CAN READ AT A GLANCE
The SP meter now changes colour as it drains: navy when full, through blue and cyan, to white when you are spent. You can tell how much magic is left without reading the number.

ALIASES SHOW UP AS YOU TYPE
When the first word you type matches one of your aliases, it turns blue in the command line, so you know it will expand before you press Enter. Works with the on-screen keyboard's text suggestions too.

QUALITY OF LIFE
• Battle filtering now catches fights with animated creatures such as the Cleaver and the Thing, whose hit lines used to slip through as ordinary text
• The Mac app now requires macOS 12 Monterey or later
