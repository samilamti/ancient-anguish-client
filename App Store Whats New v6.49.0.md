ACHIEVEMENTS
A new Achievements tab sits beside Chat, Tells, Party and Notes. Every time a skill goes up ("Your skill in Spear has gone up!"), it is logged there with the date and time, so you can look back on how your character grew. The tab lights up when something new arrives. Turn it off under Options if you prefer.

A LIGHT THEME
Pick "Paper (Light)" in the theme settings for a warm off-white page with dark ink, in the spirit of a word processor. MUD colours are re-cut for the light page so every line stays readable.

SEE WHAT CHANGED IN YOUR SCORE
When you type score, Exp and Money now show how much they moved since your last score, for example "Exp : 1,273,879 +600xp" and "Money : 30,454 coins -6,000". This works with Sheets on or off.

MAKE RULES FROM YOUR HISTORY
Select text in the History view to create a text link rule from it. In the Recent commands list, long-press or right-click a command to turn it into an alias or a text link rule, already filled in.
