ROOM INSIGHT
A new Room tab beside Chat, Tells, Party, Notes and Achievements shows the room you are standing in: its name, the exits, and every creature and object it lists. Each one comes with buttons for what you can do with it, such as "look at raven", "kill raven", "get coins" or "eat crystal". Two of the same creature are told apart as "frog" and "frog 2". The room appears a second after you stop, so walking through several rooms does not make it flicker. Turn it off under Options if you prefer.

PLAY MODE FOR THE KEYBOARD
Press Ctrl/Cmd+G to play with the keyboard instead of typing. WASD walks (hold a key to keep walking), Tab cycles through the creatures in the room, T attacks the target and the number keys fire a 10-slot action bar built from your aliases. The keys you press are shown over the output, and a new Play Mode settings pane lets you tune it.

TARGETS
Aliases can now use $target. Tapping a red kill link, choosing from the Kill picker or pressing Tab in Play Mode sets it.

SMALLER FIXES
Skill gains that report your new value are logged as achievements again, a narrow chat panel no longer cuts off its title bar, and the compass keeps its direction letters out of the way of place names.
