PLAY MODE FOR THE KEYBOARD
Press Ctrl/Cmd+P to play with the keyboard instead of typing. WASD walks (hold a key to keep walking), Tab cycles through the creatures in the room, T attacks the target and the number keys fire a 10-slot action bar built from your aliases. The keys you press are shown over the output, and a new Play Mode settings pane lets you tune it. Press Ctrl/Cmd+P again and you are back in the command line, ready to type.

TARGETS
Aliases can now use $target. Tapping a red kill link, choosing from the Kill picker or pressing Tab in Play Mode sets it.

CLEAR A WINDOW
Chat, Tells, Party and Achievements each have a clear button in their title bar. Your chat and tell history files are kept; only the window is emptied.

A TIDIER SETTINGS MENU
Settings is now a short menu: Appearance, Layout, Output, Commands and Session each open on their own page. The new Layout page gathers what sits around the output: the social windows, the compass, quick commands and the D-Pad, and which stats the HUD shows.

SMALLER FIXES
Closed every social window? Press Communications and they come back. Soulbinder skill gains ("You have increased in ... skill.") are logged as achievements, skill gains that report your new value are logged as achievements again, a narrow chat panel no longer cuts off its title bar, and the compass keeps its direction letters out of the way of place names.
